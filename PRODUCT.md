# GenerativeAI Community

## Platform
Web; existing Astro site on Cloudflare.

## Audience and purpose
People building, researching, and applying generative AI, including students and independent practitioners. The site provides community resources, events, a job board, and membership applications.

## Confirmed membership requirements
Approved plan: LinkedIn-only sign-in; short background and AI project application; automatic approval for clear practitioners and human review for uncertain cases; WhatsApp invitations sent directly by Cloudflare Email Service from noreply@genaicommunity.ai. Applicant-authorized agents may submit using a revocable 24-hour token after browser consent. Preserve existing site design and job board.
