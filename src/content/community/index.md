## About

We have over 2,000 members, mostly in Bangalore and San Francisco. Members include CTOs, senior ML engineers, and open-source hackers from Guardrails, Ragas, Llama Index, Qdrant, FastEmbed, KissanAI, Sarvam, Portkey, and other projects and startups.

Over 200 members take part each month. We discuss compute costs, vLLM, latency, and evaluations.

We have held monthly events since February, including talks, demo days, and hackathons. One hackathon ran for 24 hours. Speakers have included Amod Malviya (Flipkart/Udaan) and Prof. Pratyush (AI4Bharat/Sarvam).

Ragas became a business and joined YCombinator. Previous speaker Ravi Theja joined Llama Index, and collaborator Shivendu Kumar joined Qdrant. FastEmbed started in response to needs raised by members.

[Join the community](/apply). Sign in with LinkedIn and apply with the form or your AI agent. If approved, you will receive a WhatsApp invitation by email.

## WhatsApp community rules

The rules below mark recommended behaviour, actions that may lead to a ban, and behaviour we discourage. Context matters for discouraged behaviour.

Admins may remove conversations and remove or ban members under these rules.

## Content sharing

Recommended: Share AI research, philosophy, implementation questions, or ideas about its future. New tools and examples of how you use them are welcome.

Recommended: Give context when sharing or asking for help. Explain what you need.

May lead to a ban: Promoting your own products, company, or events. Do not use the group as a marketing channel.

Discouraged: Sharing paid or paywalled content. You can explain why it is relevant to a discussion, but sharing the content is discouraged.

## Adding friends

Recommended: Share the group link with your friends and ask them to apply: [Join](/apply)

Discouraged: Asking the main group to add a friend. Contact an admin directly.

## General conversation

Recommended: Ask questions, especially follow-ups if a conversation or topic intrigues you.

Recommended: Share what you know. Helping each other grows the AI community in India.

Recommended: Act in good faith and with integrity. When in doubt, ask.

Recommended: Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to the conversation.

May lead to a ban: Discussions of technologies with proven harm, such as deepfakes, surveillance, or technology that promotes discrimination.

May lead to a ban: Harassing or abusing a person or group.

May lead to a ban: Inactivity for more than 60 days. Inactive members will be removed from the group.

Discouraged: Off-topic discussions. Avoid spamming others' inboxes with messages irrelevant to the group. Take those discussions to DMs.

Discouraged: Sensationalism and exaggerations.

Discouraged: Asking whether you can ask a question. Ask it directly and explain what you have tried and where you need help.

## How can I promote my product/blog/event on the group?

Recommended: Have someone else share it on the group.

May lead to a ban: Promote your own product/blog/event.

## Groups

Separate topic groups keep the main channel focused. Each has its own moderators and rules:

- The GenerativeAI Group: main hub channel
- Project Demos & Events: member demos, meetup announcements
- Voice Agents: speech, TTS, voice agents
- DeepMedia: diffusion, image, video and audio generative models
- Generative AI x Security: red-teaming, jailbreaks, AI security
- Training and Inference: GPUs, training infra, inference stack
- Agents and Harnesses: agent frameworks, code harnesses
- Job Posts & Talent: hiring + candidate posts
- India AI Research: India-focused research discussions
- Coffee & Code: Papers Club BLR: Bangalore IRL paper-reading meetup
- Search: Embedding, Infra, Text: search, embeddings, retrieval infra
- Gen AI Startup Ecosystem: founders + startups in GenAI
- AI for Healthcare, Biology: health and biology
- AI for Creatives: artists, musicians, directors, writers

To join a topic group, ask an admin in the main group and briefly explain what you’re working on.
